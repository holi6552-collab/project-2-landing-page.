Baked Chicken and Rice

A Simple, Homemade Comfort Meal

Baked chicken and rice is one of my favorite meals because it is simple, filling, and easy to make. It is also a meal that reminds me of when I was younger.

About This Recipe

I chose baked chicken and rice because it has always been one of my favorite meals since I was younger. It is a nostalgic meal for me and reminds me of home.

This recipe is not the exact recipe my mom uses, but it is pretty similar. I like that the chicken and rice are cooked together, which makes the meal simple without needing a lot of different side dishes.

Ingredients

These ingredients are simple and can be found at most grocery stores.

  • Chicken breasts
  • White rice
  • Chicken broth
  • Cream of chicken soup
  • Seasonings: 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder & 1 teaspoon paprika
  • Butter
  • Parmesean cheese

Instructions

This recipe is easy to follow, even if you are new to cooking.

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Prepare the rice and chicken in a baking dish.
  3. Add the chicken broth and cream of chicken soup.
  4. OPTIONAL: shred the chicken
  5. Season the chicken and rice.
  6. Cover the dish and bake until the chicken and rice are cooked.
  7. Add the parmesean cheese as garnish.
  8. Let the meal cool for a few minutes before serving.

Why I Like This Recipe

If I could only eat one meal for the rest of my life, I would choose baked chicken and rice. I like how the creamy rice goes with the seasoned chicken.

Another reason I chose this recipe is that it is good for beginners. You do not need to make a bunch of different dishes to have a filling meal.

Tips

Keep It Simple

One of the best things about this recipe is that it does not require a lot of complicated steps. Make sure the chicken is fully cooked and the rice is tender before serving.

Adjust the Seasonings

You can adjust the seasonings based on what you like. This makes the recipe easy to change while still keeping the basic chicken and rice combination.

Try This Recipe

Baked chicken and rice is a simple, filling meal that is easy to make at home.
